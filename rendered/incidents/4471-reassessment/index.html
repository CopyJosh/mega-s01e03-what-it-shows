The shared parser reads dates month first. It can swap the day and month for day-first tenants. This group includes app and email reports, plus booking-v2 form reports.

The booking-v3 form displays MM/DD/YYYY to day-first tenants, then reads their input day first. This is a separate hint defect. Fixing the shared parser alone does not fix it. Keep these form reports in their own ticket.
